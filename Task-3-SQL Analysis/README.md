# 📊 E-Commerce Order Analysis Using SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce order data using SQL to uncover key business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, product demand, payment preferences, referral channels, and revenue generation.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence that supports data-driven decision-making and operational optimization.

---

## Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional data daily, but without proper analysis, valuable insights remain hidden.

The company needed answers to critical questions such as:

* How many orders were placed?
* Which products generate the most revenue?
* Which payment methods are most preferred by customers?
* Which referral channels drive the highest number of orders and revenue?
* What is the distribution of order statuses?
* What are the highest and lowest performing orders?
* Are there unusual order values (outliers) that require investigation?
* What factors influence overall sales performance?

---

## Business Impact

By answering these questions, stakeholders can:

* Improve revenue generation strategies.
* Optimize product offerings.
* Identify high-performing marketing channels.
* Enhance customer acquisition efforts.
* Monitor operational efficiency through order fulfillment metrics.
* Detect unusual transactions and pricing anomalies.
* Make informed business decisions using data-backed insights.

## Key Insights

### Product Performance

* Certain products consistently generate higher order volumes and revenue.
* Top-performing products contribute significantly to overall business sales.

### Revenue Trends

* Revenue is concentrated among a limited number of products.
* Delivered orders account for the majority of realized revenue.

### Customer Payment Preferences

* Specific payment methods dominate customer transactions.
* Understanding payment preferences can improve checkout experience and conversion rates.

### Marketing Effectiveness

* Some referral channels generate substantially more orders and revenue than others.
* Marketing investment can be optimized by focusing on high-performing acquisition channels.

### Order Fulfillment

* Order status analysis provides visibility into operational efficiency.
* Monitoring non-delivered orders helps identify fulfillment bottlenecks.

### Outlier Detection

* Several unusually high-value transactions were identified.
* These orders may represent premium purchases, bulk orders, or data quality issues requiring validation.

---

## Recommendations

### 1. Focus on High-Revenue Products

Allocate more marketing resources and inventory planning toward products generating the highest revenue.

### 2. Strengthen Top Referral Channels

Increase investment in referral sources that consistently drive both orders and revenue.

### 3. Optimize Payment Experience

Promote the most preferred payment methods to reduce friction during checkout.

### 4. Monitor Order Status Performance

Implement regular tracking of canceled and pending orders to improve operational efficiency.

### 5. Investigate Outlier Transactions

Review unusually high-value orders to identify opportunities, fraud risks, or pricing inconsistencies.

### 6. Expand High-Performing Product Categories

Leverage insights from top-selling products to guide future inventory and product strategy.
