# E-Commerce Sales Performance & Order Fulfillment Analysis

## Project Summary

This project analyzes e-commerce transactional data to uncover insights into sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, marketing effectiveness, payment preferences, and order fulfillment outcomes.

The analysis was conducted using Excel for data cleaning, SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS) for querying and analysis, and Power BI for dashboard development and visualization.

---

## Business Problem

The business needed to understand:

* Product performance
* Revenue drivers
* Customer payment preferences
* Marketing channel effectiveness
* Order fulfillment performance
* Customer purchasing behavior

The goal was to transform raw transaction data into actionable insights that support operational and strategic decision-making.

## Key KPIs

* Total Orders: 1,200
* Delivered Orders: 231
* Cancelled Orders: 250
* Returned Orders: 247
* Shipped Orders: 235
* Pending Orders: 237
* Total Revenue (Delivered Orders): $242,600.32
* Highest Order Value: $3,456.39
* Top Revenue Product: Laptop
* Most Ordered Product: Printer
* Top Revenue Referral Source: Email
* Most Used Payment Method: Online

---

## Key Insights

* Laptop generated the highest revenue.
* Printer recorded the highest order volume.
* Online payments generated the highest revenue contribution.
* Email marketing produced the highest revenue among referral sources.
* More than 40% of orders were either cancelled or returned, highlighting significant fulfillment challenges.
* High-value transaction analysis identified premium customer purchasing patterns.

---

## Recommendations

* Improve order fulfillment processes.
* Reduce cancellation and return rates.
* Expand investment in email marketing campaigns.
* Increase focus on high-revenue product categories.
* Strengthen digital payment adoption.
* Monitor and investigate high-value transactions.
